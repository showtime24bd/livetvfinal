  - Press 2, 5 → Channel 25
  - Screen shows preview before switching

Arrow Keys:
  - ↑ / Page Up → Previous Channel
  - ↓ / Page Down → Next Channel
  - Shows notification when changing

Media Controls:
  - Space / Play → Pause/Play video
  - F → Fullscreen toggle
  - M → Mute toggle

  - Escape/Back → Close menu/Exit fullscreen
